# Nexus Sensing Technologies - Pre-Seed Memorandum

## 01 // Silicon-Optimized Deep Biometrics & High-Security Sensors
- Process Node: 22nm FD-SOI
- Power Consumption: < 50 mW
- Signal Latency: < 50 ns (WCET)
- Target Sectors: High-Sec B2B Only

## 02 // The Market Gap: Surface Biometrics Have Hit a Wall
Conventional capacitive and optical fingerprint sensors read only the outer epidermis layer. They are vulnerable to silicone molds and dead tissue spoofing.

## 03 // Founding Team & Edge
- Product Leadership (Ex-FPC / Precise Biometrics): 20 years commercial sensor experience.
- Signal & Safety Architecture: Deep expertise in Extended Kalman filtering, closed-loop state estimation, and deterministic WCET.

## 04 // System Architecture: The Companion-ASIC Advantage
Shifting signal conditioning and Kalman/ML estimation from lab racks directly into a 2x2 mm 22nm FD-SOI silicon chip (< 50 ns loop latency).

## 05 // Deep Tissue Biometrics: Beyond the Epidermis
Sub-surface vascular structure and micro-magnetic tissue measurement providing absolute liveness detection immune to silicone or elastomer molds.

## 06 // Go-To-Market Strategy: High-Security Niches (Zero Automotive)
Targeting Institutional Crypto Wallets, Defense & Datacenter HSMs, and Portable Medical Diagnostics.
